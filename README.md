# Sarathy — Chào Linh 🌸

**The financial companion built for one person first.**

Live prototype: [https://trysarathy.github.io/sarathy-linh](https://trysarathy.github.io/sarathy-linh)

---

## What this is

This is Sarathy's first focused prototype — built around one real person, one real problem.

Linh is a Vietnamese student who arrived in Singapore for her first semester with VND in a bank account she could not access, no debit card, and no SGD. No bank helped her. No app helped her. She spent her first semester borrowing from friends, doing informal currency swaps, and figuring it out alone.

Sarathy was built to make sure that never happens again.

---

## The one problem this solves

**Day 1 currency access for international students.**

An international student arrives in Singapore with money in their home bank account but no way to access local currency. They cannot open a bank account without a local address. They cannot get a local address without paying rent. They cannot pay rent without local currency. It is a loop nobody warned them about.

Sarathy breaks that loop in four steps:
1. Live exchange rate comparison — best places to exchange VND to SGD right now
2. Peer exchange network — verified Vietnamese students with SGD who will swap at fair rates
3. Step-by-step guide to opening a Singapore bank account
4. Wise setup — so the problem never occurs again

---

## Why this person

In a primary research interview, a Vietnamese NUS student described her first semester in Singapore:

> *"I only have VND and I didn't know how to exchange that. So for a long time I have to save my money a lot. When I hang out with my friend, I will help them to pay in SGD and then pay them in VND. And when I find some of my friends who have a lot of SGD and they need to exchange back to VND to go back to Vietnam — that is the solution I have now."*

She invented peer exchange out of necessity, survived through community, and only solved the problem permanently when she flew home and opened a new account. She went through an entire semester financially stranded.

That interview is why this prototype exists.

---

## What makes Sarathy different

Every financial app treats money as the problem. Sarathy treats the person as the starting point.

Linh did not have a financial literacy problem. She had a Day 1 survival problem that no institution was designed to solve. Sarathy meets her exactly where she is — in a foreign city, far from home, overwhelmed — and walks one step at a time.

The Vietnamese language, the cultural references, the acknowledgement of homesickness, the peer network — none of these are features. They are what it looks like when a product is genuinely built for one specific person rather than for a generic user.

---

## Design decisions

**Colour palette — jade, lotus, gold**
Jade is Vietnam's stone of protection and good fortune. Lotus is the national flower — it grows through difficulty into something beautiful. Gold is warmth and home. The palette is not decorative. It is cultural.

**Language — bilingual naturally**
Vietnamese phrases are woven into the interface the way a bilingual friend would speak — not as translation, as warmth. Nhà (home). Tự do (freedom). Mình ở đây với bạn (I am here with you).

**My Story screen — a letter from Sarathy**
The most important screen in the app is not the exchange rates. It is the letter that says: *"You went through your first semester with no card, no SGD, no guide. You figured it out yourself. Sarathy was built so that the next Linh never has to do that alone."* That is what personalisation actually means.

## About Sarathy

Sarathy is an AI financial companion for international students and NRIs navigating a foreign country's financial system for the first time.

The name comes from the Sanskrit word for "charioteer" — the guide who accompanies you, who knows the terrain, who is always unconditionally on your side.

Main product: [https://trysarathy.github.io/fin-companion](https://trysarathy.github.io/fin-companion)
